# AFYALINK

## County Government Partnership

### AfyaLink
County Health Operations and Hospital Coordination Platform
Executive Summary
AfyaLink is a hospital operations and workforce platform built to improve how county health systems coordinate administration, staffing, patient flow, diagnostics, referrals, and reporting across facilities.

It helps county governments move from fragmented hospital processes to a more standardized, trackable, and scalable digital operating model.

We are seeking county-level pilot collaboration to deploy AfyaLink across selected facilities and strengthen service delivery performance.

## 1. Vision
To help county governments run more efficient, accountable, and connected health systems through a unified platform for hospital operations and workforce management.

## 2. County Health Challenges
County facilities often face:
- Fragmented administrative workflows
- Slow approvals and staffing coordination
- Limited visibility into queues, bottlenecks, and service delays
- Weak referral continuity between lower and higher-level facilities
- Inconsistent reporting across facilities
- Difficulty standardizing operational processes county-wide

These gaps reduce service quality and make health systems harder to manage at scale.

## 3. AfyaLink Solution for Counties
AfyaLink supports:
- Facility-level dashboards for day-to-day operations
- Hospital admin, HR, payroll, diagnostics, pharmacy, and patient-facing workflows
- Referral and transfer continuity patterns
- Government hospital registry and verification-friendly processes
- Training, readiness, and operational governance support
- Analytics foundations for cross-facility monitoring

## 4. County-Level Value
### 4.1 Standardized Hospital Operations
Creates repeatable operating workflows across county facilities.

### 4.2 Better Workforce Oversight
Improves staffing visibility, approvals, payroll coordination, and training readiness.

### 4.3 Better Service Delivery Monitoring
Enables clearer visibility into appointments, diagnostics turnaround, pharmacy linkage, and patient movement.

### 4.4 Stronger Referral Continuity
Supports smoother patient transfer and follow-up across hospitals and community teams.

### 4.5 Better Governance
Adds auditability, role controls, and operational evidence for accountability.

## 5. Example County Use Cases
- County referral hospital operations management
- Sub-county and county hospital coordination
- Staffing and approval workflows across facilities
- Lab, imaging, and pharmacy service monitoring
