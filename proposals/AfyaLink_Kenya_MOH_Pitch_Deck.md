# AFYALINK

## Kenya Ministry of Health Partnership

### AfyaLink
Integrated Hospital Operations, Workforce, and Patient Continuity Platform
Executive Summary
AfyaLink is a healthcare operations platform designed to connect hospital administration, clinical workflows, workforce management, diagnostics, pharmacy, billing, and inter-facility continuity in one system.

It brings together HRMS, hospital workflows, compliance controls, multi-role dashboards, and health-data interoperability so hospitals can reduce fragmentation, improve patient flow, and strengthen service delivery.

We are seeking partnership with the Government of Kenya and public health institutions to pilot AfyaLink as a digital operations backbone for hospitals, counties, and referral networks.

## 1. Vision
To help Kenya build a modern, interoperable, and locally adaptable digital health operations platform that improves care delivery, workforce efficiency, and accountability across public and private health facilities.

## 2. Problem Statement
Many health facilities still operate through fragmented systems and manual coordination, leading to:
- Delayed patient movement across departments
- Weak workforce scheduling and approval workflows
- Poor visibility into hospital performance and bottlenecks
- Fragmented lab, pharmacy, imaging, billing, and referral processes
- Limited continuity across hospitals and community follow-up teams
- Audit, compliance, and reporting gaps

Without a unified operational layer, hospitals lose time, revenue, and clinical coordination quality.

## 3. Our Solution — AfyaLink Platform
AfyaLink unifies hospital operations through:
- Role-based dashboards for admins, doctors, nurses, lab, pharmacy, HR, payroll, security, community health workers, and patients
- Appointment, consultation, admission, and referral workflows
- HR and payroll operations for healthcare teams
- Pharmacy, lab, radiology, therapy, and front-desk modules
- Audit logs, access governance, and readiness controls
- Interoperability support for structured exchange workflows including FHIR/HL7-aligned transfers

This creates one operational command layer for healthcare delivery rather than many disconnected tools.

## 4. Key Capabilities
### 4.1 End-to-End Hospital Operations
Supports patient flow from booking and reception to consultation, diagnostics, medication, follow-up, and transfer continuity.

### 4.2 Workforce and HR Management
Enables staff registration, approvals, leave flows, training readiness, payroll support, and performance visibility.

### 4.3 Multi-Hospital Administration
Supports hospital groups, branch-level oversight, and transfer-aware workflows for continuity between facilities.

### 4.4 Compliance and Control
Includes auditability, route access governance, feature controls, readiness gates, backup/failover planning, and operational evidence packs.

### 4.5 Offline and Resilience Readiness
Includes offline operations monitoring, operational drill support, and observability assets for resilient healthcare delivery.

## 5. National Strategic Value
### 5.1 Better Public Hospital Efficiency
Improves queue management, staff coordination, departmental throughput, and response times.

### 5.2 Stronger Patient Continuity
Supports better referrals, transfer packages, and follow-up visibility across facilities.

### 5.3 Workforce Accountability
Gives administrators clearer staffing, approvals, training, payroll, and audit visibility.

### 5.4 Digital Health Standardization
Helps establish repeatable hospital operating models that can scale county by county.

### 5.5 Local Adaptability
Built around Kenyan healthcare operational realities, including multi-role hospital workflows and low-friction administration.

## 6. Public-Sector Use Cases
- County referral hospital operations management
- National and county hospital workforce administration
- Public hospital queue and appointment optimization
- Pharmacy and diagnostics turnaround monitoring
- Community health worker follow-up coordination
- Government hospital registry and verification workflows
- Regulatory and operational reporting support

- Audit logs and traceability

### Phase 2 — County Expansion

### Phase 3 — National Network

- Inter-facility continuity and transfer exchange
- Operational benchmarking and command-center visibility
