# Hypercare Playbook (First 30 Days)

## Daily Cadence
- Morning status review
- Midday defect triage
- End-of-day KPI and risk review

## Priority Queue
1. Patient safety and clinical workflow blockers
2. Data integrity and integration failures
3. Role/permission access defects
4. UX and performance issues

## Exit Criteria
- No Sev1 incidents for 14 consecutive days
- Sev2 MTTR below 4 hours for 2 weeks
- Connector success rate above 99%
- User adoption KPIs stable by role
