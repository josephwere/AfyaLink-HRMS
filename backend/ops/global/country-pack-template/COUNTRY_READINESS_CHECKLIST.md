- [ ] Privacy and health law assessment complete
- [ ] Licensing and regulator engagement started
- [ ] Contract templates localized

## Data Residency
- [ ] Residency obligations defined
- [ ] Hosting strategy approved
- [ ] Cross-border transfer controls documented

## Technical Integration
- [ ] National insurance/government interfaces mapped
- [ ] HL7/FHIR compatibility matrix complete
- [ ] Pilot hospitals shortlisted

## Security and Operations
- [ ] Incident process localized
- [ ] On-call and escalation contacts assigned
- [ ] SLO dashboard and alerts operational

## Launch Readiness
- [ ] Pilot wave plan approved
- [ ] Training content localized
- [ ] Hypercare support model staffed
