# Compliance and Data Residency Matrix

## Principle
Healthcare data must be handled with least privilege, lawful basis, auditable access, and country-specific residency constraints.

## Control Matrix
- Identity and access: MFA/step-up, RBAC + ABAC, session risk controls.
- Data protection: encryption in transit and at rest, key rotation, token scoping.
- Auditability: immutable logs, actor/resource/time correlation, export provenance.
- Consent: patient-controlled consent scopes for transfer/export.
- Retention: policy-based retention and legal hold handling.

## Country Rollout Checklist (per country)
1. Legal basis and privacy law mapping complete.
2. Data residency requirement confirmed.
3. Government reporting interfaces validated.
4. Incident breach notification timelines documented.
5. Cross-border transfer restrictions implemented.

## Evidence Pack
- Access control policy export
- Audit log samples
- Encryption/key-management evidence
- DR test report
- Vulnerability scan and remediation report
